The Usual

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

The short version

  • Your phone number is used only to sign you in. It is never shown to anyone.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies. We don't sell your information.
  • We never share your phone number or text-message consent with anyone for marketing.
  • You choose who sees what, and you can delete your account at any time.

The Usual (“we”, “us”) is run by Theile Riordan in Florida, United States. This policy explains what information The Usual collects at gettheusual.bar, how it is used, and the choices you have. Questions? Email cheers@gettheusual.bar.

What we collect

Everyone

  • Mobile phone number, to send you a one-time sign-in code by text message.
  • Display name you choose.
  • Who you follow: the bartenders you're connected with, and how you connected (QR code, spoken code or link).
  • Reactions and replies you leave on bartenders' updates.

Regulars (optional)

  • A profile photo, “my usual” (your go-to drink), and your birthday as a month and day only. We never ask for your birth year.

Bartenders

  • Your photo, bio, the bars you work at and their areas, your time zone, and your schedule.
  • Updates you post, with any photos, and who each one is visible to.
  • Private notes you keep about your regulars, and the people you block.

Collected automatically

  • Invite attempts: when someone enters an invite code, we record the account, the time, whether it worked and the IP address, to stop people guessing codes.
  • Service logs: our hosting and database providers keep standard logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) for security and troubleshooting.
  • Browser storage: The Usual stores your sign-in session and a few settings (for example, whether you're in bartender or regular mode) in your browser's local storage. These are not tracking cookies, and they never leave your device except to keep you signed in.

Photos: before a photo is uploaded, your browser resizes it and re-saves it, which strips location (GPS) and other hidden camera data.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To run the app: show regulars where their bartender is working, deliver updates, reactions and replies, and show bartenders their regulars.
  • To enforce privacy choices and blocks, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Who can see what

  • Your phone number: nobody, including bartenders and other regulars.
  • A regular's profile (photo, usual, birthday): only the bartenders that regular follows. Other regulars never see it.
  • A bartender's schedule: their regulars, or any signed-in person with their link, depending on the bartender's setting.
  • Updates: “Only my regulars” updates are seen only by that bartender's regulars. “Public” updates can also be read by signed-in people with the bartender's link.
  • Replies: private between the regular who wrote them and the bartender.
  • Reactions: regulars see counts. The bartender sees who reacted.
  • Private notes: only the bartender who wrote them.
  • Blocking: a blocked person loses access to that bartender's page, posts, schedule and photos. They aren't told.

The Usual requires you to be signed in to see anyone's page, and its pages aren't listed in search engines, apart from the public home page and pages like this one.

Text messages (SMS)

When you enter your number and tap “Text me a code”, you agree to receive a one-time sign-in code by text. We only send codes you ask for: no marketing or promotional messages. Message frequency depends on how often you sign in. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out (you won't be able to sign in by text while opted out) or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-message opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party, except the service providers below that deliver the messages.

Service providers

We use a small number of companies to run The Usual. They process information only on our behalf and under their own security and privacy commitments:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Twilio: sending sign-in codes by text message.
  • Vercel: hosting the website.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of people using The Usual.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your photos are removed first, then your profile and everything you added (updates, schedule, bars, reactions, replies, notes, connections and invite attempts). Service providers may keep logs and backups for a limited time before they are overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your name, photo, usual, birthday, bio and bars at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account at the bottom of “My profile” (or “My profile & bars” for bartenders). It's immediate and can't be undone.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing cheers@gettheusual.bar. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. We'll honour these requests wherever you are.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules enforce who can see what, so privacy settings and blocks are checked on our servers, not just in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you if a breach affects you, as required by law.

Age

The Usual is for adults: you must be 21 or older in the United States, or 18 or older (or the legal drinking age where you live, if higher) elsewhere. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think a minor has signed up, email us and we'll delete the account.

Where your information is processed

The Usual is run from the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll let you know in the app before it takes effect.
