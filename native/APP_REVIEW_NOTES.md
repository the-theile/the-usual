The Usual lets bartenders tell their regulars where they're working tonight, without sharing a
phone number or social media. A bartender adds the bars they work at and their shifts, invites
regulars with a QR code, a spoken daily code or a link, and posts short updates. Regulars see
who's working tonight and can react or reply privately. It's for adults of legal drinking age
(21+ in the US) and free, with no purchases. The app does not sell or deliver alcohol.

SIGNING IN
Sign-in is by SMS code to a phone number (no password). Use the demo phone number and fixed
code in the Sign-in fields; no real text is sent. Tick both boxes (legal drinking age, and
agreeing to the Terms), tap "Text me a code", then enter the code. The demo account
[is a bartender with bars, shifts and updates, and also follows another bartender], so both
sides of the app have content.

NATIVE FEATURES (beyond the website)
- [Push notifications: "your bartender is working tonight" — add when shipped]
- Universal Links: invite links, QR codes and bartender pages open in the app
- Camera for profile and update photos
- Offline screen when there's no connection

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (Guideline 1.2)
- Terms: everyone must tick "I agree to the Terms" (zero tolerance for objectionable content
  and abusive users) before an account is created or signed in.
- Report: a Report button on every update, on reply messages, on a bartender's page and on a
  regular's page, with reasons such as harassment, safety and inappropriate content.
- Block: a regular blocks a bartender from the bartender's page (Block); a bartender blocks a
  regular from My regulars → the regular → Block. The blocked person's content disappears for
  the blocker immediately, and every block notifies our moderators.
- Filter: names, bios, bar names, updates, replies and other text containing blocked terms are
  rejected by the server before they're saved.
- Moderation: reports go to a moderator queue in the app, where we remove content and suspend
  accounts. We act on reports within [24 hours].
- Contact: [support email]

ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v))
Regulars: My profile → Delete my account (bottom of the screen). Bartenders: My bartender
profile & bars → Delete my account. It permanently erases the profile, photos and account in the
app; no email or website step is needed.

PRIVACY
Privacy Policy is linked on the sign-in screen and at gettheusual.bar/privacy. Phone numbers are
never shown to anyone. No tracking and no third-party ad or analytics SDKs.

AGE RATING
Frequent alcohol references (bars and drinks); an age confirmation is required at sign-in.

PAYMENTS
None. The app is free and has no purchases, tips or external payment links.
